# Branches of Intellectual Work

## Scientific

### Philosophy

---

**`Metaphysics`** := The study of existence and reality, exploring its most general features such as idealism/materialism, objects and their properties, matter and mind, order/entropy, systems, and information.

---

**`Axiology`** := The study of value and valuation.

---

**`Epistemology`** := The study of what knowledge is and how we acquire it. Related: Upper ontology.

---

**`Aesthetics`** := The study of art and beauty, seeking to understand what makes art what it is and whether beauty is truly in the eye of the beholder.

---

**`Ethics`** := The study of moral principles, rules, and codes of conduct that categorize behaviors, intentions, decisions, and actions as proper (right) or improper (wrong). *See: Trolley Dilemma.*

---

**`Politics`** := The study that takes ethics to a larger scale, applying it to groups of people, their relationships, influence, and power, including governments, laws, justice, authority, rights, liberty, sustainability, and ecology.

### Mathematics

---

**`Mathematics`** := The study of properties of pure abstract entities, given under certain axioms (see MSC2010 classification).

---

**`Logic`** := The study of valid inference, proof, and formal systems.

---

**`Algebra`** := The study of structures, operations, and their general relations.

---

**`Analysis`** := The study of continuity, limits, change, and infinite processes.

---

**`Geometry`** := The study of shape, space, and their transformations.

---

**`Statistics`** := The study of collection, analysis, and interpretation of data under uncertainty.

### Information

---

**`Information Theory`** := The study of quantification, storage, and communication of information.

---

**`Computer Science`** := The study of computation, algorithms, and data structures.

---

**`Cybernetics`** := The study of control, feedback, and communication in systems.

---

**`Systems Theory`** := The study of organization, emergence, and behavior of complex systems.

### Nature

---

**`Physics`** := The study of matter, energy, and the fundamental forces of the universe.

---

**`Chemistry`** := The study of substances, their composition, properties, and transformations.

---

**`Biology`** := The study of living organisms and the processes of life.

---

**`Astronomy`** := The study of celestial objects, space, and the universe at large.

---

**`Earth Science`** := The study of the planet's structure, atmosphere, and natural processes.

### Technology

---

**`Engineering`** := The application of scientific knowledge to design and build structures, machines, and processes.

---

**`Medicine`** := The application of knowledge to the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of disease.

---

**`Agriculture`** := The application of knowledge to the cultivation of plants and raising of animals.

---

**`Computing`** := The application of knowledge to build hardware, software, and information systems.

## Non-Scientific Branches

Domains of human knowledge and expression that do not rely on the `Scientific Method`, yet shape culture, meaning, and shared life.

### Society

---

**`Culture`** := The shared customs, beliefs, rituals, and ways of life of a people.

---

**`History`** := The narrative interpretation of past human events and their meaning.

---

**`Law`** := The codified rules, norms, and institutions that govern conduct.

---

**`Religion`** := Systems of faith, worship, and the search for the sacred or transcendent.

---

**`Tradition`** := The transmission of values, practices, and identity across generations.

### Literature

---

**`Poetry`** := The art of evoking meaning and emotion through rhythm, imagery, and language.

---

**`Prose`** := The art of storytelling and reflection in fiction and non-fiction.

---

**`Drama`** := The art of representing human conflict and character through performance.

---

**`Rhetoric`** := The art of persuasion and effective expression in speech and writing.

---

**`Criticism`** := The interpretation, evaluation, and study of literary works.

### Art

---

**`Visual Arts`** := Painting, sculpture, drawing, and other forms of visual expression.

---

**`Music`** := The art of organizing sound, rhythm, and harmony to evoke emotion.

---

**`Performing Arts`** := Theatre, dance, and live forms that express through the body and presence.

---

**`Design`** := The art of shaping objects, spaces, and experiences with form and purpose.

---

**`Crafts`** := The skilled making of objects valued for beauty, utility, and tradition.

### Personal Growth

---

**`Self-Knowledge`** := The reflective understanding of one's own values, motives, strengths, and limits.

---

**`Discipline`** := The cultivation of habits, focus, and consistency toward chosen goals.

---

**`Mindfulness`** := The practice of present awareness, attention, and emotional regulation.

---

**`Skill Mastery`** := The deliberate practice and refinement of competence in a chosen craft.

---

**`Wellbeing`** := The care of body, mind, and relationships for a balanced and meaningful life.
