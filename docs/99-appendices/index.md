Correspondences from the formal core to the classical disciplines it recovers (`01`–`07`), and the notation of the Azon formulas (`08`).

- [Correspondence from Sets to Logic of Predicates](01-set-to-predicates.md) — Predicate logic recovered from Set: a Predicate as a truth-test, and the connectives and quantifiers as Set operations.
- [Correspondence from Graphs to Category Theory](02-graph-to-categories.md) — Category theory recovered from Graph: Category, Object, Morphism, Monoid, Functor, and Natural Transformation as Azons under Composition.
- [Correspondence to Arithmetic](03-numerals-to-arithmetic.md) — Arithmetic recovered from Number, Interval, Tuple, and Concatenation rather than introduced as a new primitive.
- [Correspondence to Combinatorics](04-numerals-to-combinatorics.md) — Counting over finite Sets and Tuples: every classical count reduced to the Cardinality of a constructed Set.
- [Fundamental Data Structures](05-data-structures.md) — The data structures of computing as Azons already available from Set, Graph, and Numerals, each narrowed by one constraint.
- [Common Structures](06-common-structures.md) — The standard algebras built on Structure — setoids, magmas, ringoids, and lattices.
- [Correspondence to Data Normalization](06-data-normalization.md) — The relational normal forms 1NF to BCNF stated over Schema, Type, Presentation, and Relation.
- [Branches of Intellectual Work](07-science-branches.md) — A map of the scientific, applied, and humanistic disciplines, each given by what it studies.
- [Azon Notation](08-azon-notation.md) — The formal grammar of the Azon formulas: literal, pattern and response, parameters, application, composition, relations, and the layout of a definition.
