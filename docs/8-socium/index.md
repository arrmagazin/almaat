# Socium

Society as a self-perpetuating system, and the individual's standing within it: binding → mind → contact → self → failure → power.

- [Socium](30-socium.md) — Humans do not live alone: society is a self-perpetuating system with its own logic, which reproduces through Culture, regulates through Contract, and uses individuals as carriers.
- [Ecology](31-ecology.md) — Ecology is a sustainable social Contract — one that protects individual rights, renews the conditions for development, and leaves room for new meaning.
- [Culture](32-culture.md) — Culture as the shared archetypes, symbols, and active ideas that form a group's cognitive foundation, and the extraction economy built on its carriers.
- [Influence](33-interpersonal.md) — Society improves two ways — constrain evil and compel good from outside, or work on yourself and provide the example. The second is primary.
- [Identity](34-identity.md) — Identity is the continuous story a person tells about themselves, in which boundaries, self-esteem, and the imperatives to follow are formed and maintained.
- [Social Trances](35-transes.md) — Most people carry a near-constant, invisible sense that something is wrong with them — the Inferiority Trance, and the impostor pattern it produces.
- [Conformism and Moral Courage](36-conformism.md) — Conformism is not a neutral position but an active choice with a cost; Moral Courage is its opposite — acting on your own judgment against social pressure.
- [Power Architecture](37-politics.md) — Politics is the distribution of power through non-violent means via social institutions — status is its hidden currency.
