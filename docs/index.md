The book builds one ontology from a single primitive (the `Azon`) up through the formal layer, dynamics, and mind, then applies it to the human, the disciplines of mastery, the collective, and the body. The appendices recover the classical disciplines from that formal core.

ALMAAT is a general-purpose framework for cognitive modeling: one ontology built from a single primitive, then applied to the human, the collective, and daily practice.

## 01 — Math · Foundations

The core: the structures built directly on the `Azon`.

- [Azon](01-math/00-azon.md) — Azon and core primitives of the entire framework.
- [Sets, Equivalence, Topology](01-math/01-sets.md) — Set and its Elements, the equivalence relations that compare Azons, and the topological structures over Collections of Sets.
- [Composition](01-math/02-composition.md) — The Arrow and the Composition that feeds one Azon's output into another — associativity, bijection, wel-consumption, Identity, Null.
- [Graph](01-math/03-graphs.md) — Graph as a Set of Arrows, the Vertex types classified by in/out connectivity, the Path and Tree built on them, and what makes a Graph connected.
- [Numerals](01-math/04-numerals.md) — Natural numbers (Number), rationals (Ratio, Rational), intervals, sequences, and selections.
- [Spaces](01-math/05-spaces.md) — The Space as a Tuple of Axis, and the Cluster, Point, Volume, and Form built on it.
- [Structures](01-math/06-structures.md) — The Structure: a Set of Dependencies under a Law — Dependency, Key, Operation, and the Laws that constrain them.
- [Formal Language](01-math/08-language.md) — How a Language is built from binary Code up through Alphabet, Word and Dictionary, the Rules and Grammar that transform them, the Theory derivable from an Axiom, and the four nested classes of Grammar.
- [Probability](01-math/09-probability.md) — The SampleSpace and its Events, the Probability Measure and Distribution over them, conditioning up to Bayes' Theorem, Random Variables with their moments, and the limit laws over a growing Sample.

## 02 — Reality · Dynamics & Metaphysics

How static primitives become living systems that act, and how `Reality` itself is read: Process → System → Entropy → Evolution → Being.

- [Matter](02-reality/01-matter.md) — Matter as a Tuple of Attributes read over a common Time, and the Attribution, Entity, Content, Keys, Schema, and Quality built on it.
- [Dynamic Processes](02-reality/02-process.md) — The Process as a controlled Queue of Words: its Circuit, the kinds of Circuit, the landmarks of a Flow, and what a Flow settles into over many Steps.
- [Systems](02-reality/07-system.md) — Things as queues of presentations over a flow: the participants, the communication that couples them, and what the whole exhibits that no part does.
- [Entropy](02-reality/14-entropy.md) — Entropy is the bridge between the State of a System and the passage of Steps: Order is never free, and a System holds Entropy back only by drawing Communication from its Environment.
- [Evolution](02-reality/15-evolution.md) — Evolution is the dynamics of a Paradigm: how it improves, adapts, stagnates, or regresses as Circumstances shift.
- [Limits](02-reality/16-limits.md) — What bounds development: the Communication an Agent is free of, the Constraint it navigates, and the ceiling that Improvement approaches and Breakthrough escapes.
- [Being](02-reality/22-being.md) — Being as three sides — perceptive Sensation, creative Consciousness, and representative Reality — and why Reality is a readout rather than the source.
- [Transcendence](02-reality/24-transcendence.md) — One Consciousness dissociated into many points of view: the brain is its appearance, time and space are the filing system it generates, and a choice reveals what you are.
- [The Given and the Constructed](02-reality/27-given.md) — Start from what is actually given: the perceived world is small and the universe is inferred, so the governing question is not what exists but what matters.

## 03 — Mind · Knowing

The framework's cognitive core: Content → Form → Mechanism → Process → Purpose → Capacity → Substrate → Method.

- [Knowledge](03-mind/05-knowledge.md) — The atomic units of belief — Opinion, Doubt, Thesis, Theory, Name, Concept — and the forms Knowledge is organized into.
- [Proof](03-mind/06-proof.md) — The formal apparatus that carries a Thesis to a Proof — formalism, interpretation, satisfaction, the three bounds every logic runs into, and type theory, where a proof is a program.
- [Computation](03-mind/07-computation.md) — Reasoning mechanized: Data carried through Transformers by a Runtime, expressed as a Program, prescribed by an Algorithm, and judged against a Specification.
- [Reasoning](03-mind/08-reasoning.md) — Generating new a priori Knowledge from existing Knowledge — the Inference path, and the nine modes a Mind reasons in.
- [Agency & Teleology](03-mind/09-teleos.md) — The anatomy of intentional action: who the Agent is, what drives motion toward a Future, what means are deployed, and how the outcome is judged.
- [Intellect](03-mind/11-intellect.md) — The capacity to form, validate, and apply one's own Reality — cognition, validation, learning, skill, bias, and judgment under uncertainty.
- [Mind](03-mind/12-mind.md) — Mind as a System of Neuron and Sensor — thought as state, memory as disposition, and the social origin of the inner voice.
- [The Scientific Method](03-mind/90-scientific.md) — The empirical route to Scientific Knowledge: the nine criteria a claim must meet, and the five phases that produce it.
