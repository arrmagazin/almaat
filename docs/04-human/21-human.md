Human Being *(Qalavelk)*
: creature, composed of biological circuitry, social membership, cultural inheritance, and transcendent backgrounds, powered by `Intellect`, `Inspiration` and pursuing `Mission`.

---

Inspiration *(Nadxnenne)*
: the innate drive, courage and curiosity to push to any limits - and beyond.

> Grow above yourself, whatever that means — do whatever you want, just don't stand still!
>
> Great men are not born great, they grow great. — Mario Puzo
>
> What do you want here, accursed one, and where would you have us embark? Do not lead us out of our Egyptian darkness into the light!
>
> We neurasthenic, ill-tempered apes will go on — not out of hope, and not out of a dream, but because we do not know how to do otherwise.
>
> A human being cannot stop: wherever there is an emptiness, a boundary, or a frontier, they will try to breach and cross it, without even a clear answer as to why or for what. Because they can, because it is a dare, because it is beautiful.

---

Dignity *(Godnost)*
: the worth of a `Human Being` — grounded in adaptability, long-term development, cognitive power, empathy, and the ability to cooperate, but above all in its `Inspiration` and `Intellect` in pursuing `Truth.`

---

Conscience *(Sumlenne)*
: considering each `Human Being` as one of the entire Mankind, which entails:

- respect for one's own `Dignity` and recognition the `Dignity` of each individual in the common movement toward a bright future in which everyone has realized their potential in own .
- to accept the natural right to define `Hapiness`, look for it own path, and pursue their own `Goal`s, as long as they do not infringe on the `Dignity` of others.
- and to expect comprehensive mutual support, favor, and trust from other conscientious people in return for the same.

> `Conscience` grants the right, and the obligation, to fully realize one's potential.

---

Kapot *(Qerta)*
: the boundary beyond which a person irreversibly abandons their `Dignity`.

As long as a person maintains their own attitude, holds even the smallest `Goal`, and moves toward it — the line has not been crossed. What death measures is whether that line was held: see [The Self and Death](29-self.md#death).

> If a person understands what they live for, they can endure very much, even everything. — Viktor Frankl
>
> If you don't have the strength to win, then at least have the strength not to bow. — Dzhokhar Dudayev
>
> To remain oppressed but human, rather than a walking corpse, one must realize where the Line passes, beyond which one cannot retreat under any circumstances, even if it costs Life. If you survived at the cost of crossing that line, you will continue a life that has lost all meaning. — Bettelheim
>
> Those who believed they could not influence their situation at all became literally walking corpses. First, a person rejected any attempt to make sense of their own behavior and stopped acting by their own will: there was no internal source of movement left in them. Everything they did was determined by external pressure, they automatically executed any orders, without any selectivity. Then they began to shuffle very characteristically. Then they began to look only straight ahead. And then came death. — Bettelheim
>
> Those who survived understood that they possess the last, but perhaps the most important human freedom — to choose their own attitude toward what is happening. Where there is no personal attitude, the zombie begins. — Bettelheim
