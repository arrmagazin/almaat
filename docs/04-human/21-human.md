# Human Being

**`Human Being`** := creature, composed of biological circuitry, social membership, cultural inheritance, and transcedent backgrounds, powered by `Intellect` and `Inspiration`.

---

**`Inspiration`** := the drive, courage and curiosity to push to any limits - and beyond. [[4,5,6,7,46]]

---

**`Dignity`** := the worth of a `Human Being` — grounded in adaptability, long-term development, cognitive power, empathy, and the ability to cooperate, but above all in its `Inspiration` and `Intellect` in pursuing `Truth.`

---

**`Conscience`** := considering each `Human Being` as one of the entire Mankind, which entails:

- respect for one's own `Dignity` and recognition the `Dignity` of each individual in the common movement toward a bright future in which everyone has realized their potential in own .
- to accept the natural right to define `Hapiness`, look for it own path, and pursue their own `Goal`s, as long as they do not infringe on the `Dignity` of others.
- and to expect comprehensive mutual support, favor, and trust from other conscientious people in return for the same.

> `Conscience` grants the right, and the obligation, to fully realize one's potential.
