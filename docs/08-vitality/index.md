The body as the physical foundation of health and performance (`02`–`06`).

- [Vitality](02-vitality.md) — The physical foundation of human life, health, and performance: the body's ability to function optimally, resist disease, and recover from stress.
- [Recovery](03-recovery.md) — Recovery is not idleness: sleep cleans the brain, light sets the clock, and breath and stillness switch off the stress response.
- [Nutrition](04-nutrition.md) — What you drink, what you eat, and when — the levers that set blood sugar, insulin, the gut flora, and the fuel a working day runs on.
- [Energy](05-energy.md) — Health is the sound allocation of a finite energy budget: training raises its capacity, chronic stress drains it, and burnout and depression are what its collapse feels like.
- [Sexuality](06-sexuality.md) — How desire and orgasm work, what keeps sex alive in a long relationship, and what age changes.
