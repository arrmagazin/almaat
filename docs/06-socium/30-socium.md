# Socium

## A System of People

A society is a [`System`](../1-reality/07-system.md) whose `Participants` are people. It persists because the `Communication` coupling them persists, not because any particular person does — every member is replaced within a lifetime and the thing itself carries on.

That independence is what gives it a logic of its own, and two mechanisms carry it. It reproduces through [Culture](32-culture.md), whose `Collective Mind` sets the bounds of thought before any thought inside it is examined. It regulates through the social [`Contract`](31-ecology.md), an allocation of `Roles` around a shared objective, which says which moves are permitted and at what cost.

Neither is chosen by the people it forms. Both outlast them.
