# Culture

## Collective Mind

**`Collective Mind`** := the mentality of a group — invisible to those inside it, it sets the bounds of thought before any thought is examined.

### Idemons

**`Idemon`** := a self-replicating idea that spreads by offering relief from the cost of thinking for oneself.

Idemons spread through language, ritual, and media by offering:

- Simple answers — replace complex thinking.
- Ready purpose — substitute personal meaning.
- No responsibility — remove burden of self-direction.
- Group identity — inflate sense of significance.

A mind without its own truth becomes a host for external ideas.

### Crowd

When individuals dissolve into a collective, rational deliberation stops and the crowd acts as one organism toward external targets. Neurochemical bonding reinforces in-group solidarity and out-group aggression. Rapid collective coordination was an evolutionary advantage — but crowd intelligence is sub-individual, not super-individual.

### Enemy Image

The oldest brain structures assemble an "enemy" before conscious thought engages: fear and anger fire ahead of the prefrontal cortex. For our ancestors, mistaking kin for a predator cost less than missing a real predator, so the system is tuned toward false positives.

As frontal lobes grew alongside social complexity, the us/them split became cognitive shorthand — a generalized enemy lets the mind decide without examining each individual, and stereotype is the price of that speed. The enemy image is not a malfunction but the residue of tribal adaptation, and a shared enemy remains among the strongest accelerants of solidarity and mobilization.

## Cultural Transmission

- **Heritage** — fixed deposits: language, myth, art, customs, institutions.
- **Education** — direct transfer of existing knowledge to new carriers.
- **Propaganda** — systematic insertion of ideas into mass consciousness through repetition and emotional triggers.
- **Religion** — comprehensive idea packages that satisfy existential needs and direct collective behavior.

## Extraction

Society does not only transmit culture — it **extracts** from its carriers. Wherever the [Animal](../4-human/28-animal.md) has a vulnerability, a market forms to monetize it. The product is engineered to capture, not to serve.

- **Attention** — hours and focus, resold to advertisers. Feeds, autoplay, and notifications are tuned to defeat your stopping point. See [Attention](../7-mastery/07-attention.md).
- **Dopamine** — the reward circuit, hooked by variable payoff — games, scrolling, betting. Designed compulsion sold as entertainment. See [Conditioning](../8-sustain/09-manipulation.md#conditioning).
- **Status** — manufactured insecurity, resold as goods that signal worth: cars, clothes, weddings. The need is created, then the cure is priced.
- **Appetite** — cheap, engineered food tuned past the body's satiety signal. See [Nutrition](../9-vitality/04-nutrition.md).

**Optimized for the next click, not your good.** An extractive feed does not show what you like — it shows what keeps you going. Because [novelty renews arousal](../4-human/28-animal.md#reward-and-compulsion) (the Coolidge effect), maximizing engagement tends toward steadily more stimulating content.

**The tell.** The pitch promises a result without resistance: easy money, permanent flow, a life that arranges itself. Capability is built at the edge of what one cannot yet do (see [Learning → Deliberate Practice](../7-mastery/09-learning.md#deliberate-practice)), so an offer of results without resistance is describing the bait, not the method.

The defense mirrors the one against [manipulation](../8-sustain/09-manipulation.md): make consumption **deliberate, not reflexive**. An appetite you did not choose is one someone else is steering.
