How static primitives become living systems that act, and how `Reality` itself is read: Process → System → Entropy → Evolution → Being.

- [Matter](01-matter.md) — Matter as a Tuple of Attributes read over a common Time, and the Attribution, Entity, Content, Keys, Schema, and Quality built on it.
- [Dynamic Processes](02-process.md) — The Process as a controlled Queue of Words: its Circuit, the kinds of Circuit, the landmarks of a Flow, and what a Flow settles into over many Steps.
- [Systems](07-system.md) — Things as queues of presentations over a flow: the participants, the communication that couples them, and what the whole exhibits that no part does.
- [Entropy](14-entropy.md) — Entropy is the bridge between the State of a System and the passage of Steps: Order is never free, and a System holds Entropy back only by drawing Communication from its Environment.
- [Evolution](15-evolution.md) — Evolution is the dynamics of a Paradigm: how it improves, adapts, stagnates, or regresses as Circumstances shift.
- [Limits](16-limits.md) — What bounds development: the Communication an Agent is free of, the Constraint it navigates, and the ceiling that Improvement approaches and Breakthrough escapes.
- [Being](22-being.md) — Being as three sides — perceptive Sensation, creative Consciousness, and representative Reality — and why Reality is a readout rather than the source.
- [Transcendence](24-transcendence.md) — One Consciousness dissociated into many points of view: the brain is its appearance, time and space are the filing system it generates, and a choice reveals what you are.
- [The Given and the Constructed](27-given.md) — Start from what is actually given: the perceived world is small and the universe is inferred, so the governing question is not what exists but what matters.
