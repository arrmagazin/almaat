## External Bounds

Freedom *(Svaboda)*
: a `Participant`'s independence from `Communication` that would coerce a change of its `Paradigm`.

*NOTE*: the ability to act on one's own `Plan` without coercion from the `Environment`. Always conditional and relative to specified `Communication` — never absolute.

---

Constraint *(Skuta)*
: the set of `Communication` that limit the range of accessible `Plan` for a `Participant` `A`.

> `Constraint<A> { c -> (Accessible<A,Z> ⊄ Accessible<A,c>)[c] }`

*NOTE*: `Accessible<A,c>` is the `Set` of `Plan`s open to `A` under the `Communication` `c`, and `Accessible<A,Z>` the ones open with none: `c` limits `A` when some `Plan` open without it is closed under it.

*NOTE*: dual to `Freedom`. Growth occurs through navigating constraints, not by eliminating them. Useful `Constraint` becomes the scaffold of [`Mastery`](../05-mastery/01-mastery.md).

## The Ceiling

Excellence *(Daskanalost)*
: the ceiling of `Potency` achievable within a given `Paradigm` `P`, under `Circumstances` `C`.

> `Excellence<P,C> = max p ∈ Plans<P>: Potency<p,P,C>`

**DEF**: the `Plan`s a `Paradigm` generates, for any `System`: `Plans<P> = ⋃<COD<P>>`.

*NOTE*: a *relative* ceiling, not absolute: a new `Paradigm` can extend it, after which the prior `Excellence` becomes a plateau.

---

Improvement *(Dhivna)*
: intention toward `Excellence` *within* the bounds of the current `Paradigm` `P`: from a `Plan` `p` to a better `Plan` `q` of the same `Paradigm`, under `Circumstances` `C`.

> `Improvement<P,p,q,C> :: {p, q} ⊆ Plans<P>, Potency<p,P,C> < Potency<q,P,C> ≤ Excellence<P,C>`

*NOTE*: the gradient of effort inside an unchanged `Paradigm` — incremental refinement. Produces depth, not novelty.

---

Breakthrough *(Dxarna)*
: intention to escape the current `Paradigm` `P` into a new one `Q`.

> `Breakthrough<P,Q> :: ![Q IS P]`

*NOTE*: often follows [`Crisis`](15-evolution.md) — when `Improvement` is no longer sufficient, `Breakthrough` becomes the only remaining vector.
