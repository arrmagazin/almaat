# The Given and the Constructed

An infant is shown the world; only later does the `Mind` name it "universe" and bind it into a picture. Your adult perception is that same given, now labeled — you see no more than the infant, you only know more.

## What Is Given

- **The world you perceive is small; the universe is inferred.** What is directly given is the space immediately around you, bounded by your own life. The far cosmos, the deep past, the far future — none of it is perceived. It is a construct assembled from science, testimony, and habit: reliable-looking, yet unperceived.
- **Its age is your age — or a few hours.** The manifest world appears when you become self-aware and ends when you depart. On this reading it is remade after each sleep, which interrupts the given.
- **Causality is imposed, not found.** Direct experience contains only phenomena; the causal links that let us infer a vast external world are added by the `Mind`. The mind-independent, billion-year universe is therefore a mental construct — and consensus is not truth.
- **The evidence of the past is on the screen.** Chronicles, fossils, and artifacts do not sit *beside* you as independent proof; you perceive them *now*, as part of the present given, so they cannot serve as independent confirmation of it. There is one viewer: you.

## Precedents

- **Berkeley** — *esse est percipi*: to be is to be perceived.
- **Hume** — causal connection is supplied by the mind, not found in the phenomena.
- **Schopenhauer** — only the present is actually existent.
- **Augustine** — the three times exist only in the soul: memory, attention, expectation.
- **Husserl** — *epoché*, the deliberate suspension of inherited cosmology and "common sense" so the given can be seen plainly.

## The Manifest and the Existent

Sharpen the terms: the "I" *exists but is not manifest* — you never find it as an object in experience, yet nothing appears without it. The world *is manifest but, without an "I", does not exist* — appearance requires someone to whom it appears.

This is not solipsism: the manifest is `Being` disclosing itself, and it is shared and mutually agreed across subjects — [one `Consciousness`, many dissociations](24-transcendence.md#one-consciousness).

## The Redirection

The far cosmos and deep past are optional constructions that do not touch how you live. The governing question is therefore not *what exists* or *what is fundamental* but **what matters** — what you are, and why life is given.

Improving the outer world never improves the state of the "I"; changing the "I" reorders every value.
