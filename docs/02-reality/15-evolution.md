What bounds those trajectories is in [Limits](16-limits.md).

## Trajectories

Evolution *(Razvoj)*
: a change of `Paradigm` `P` into `Q` toward strictly greater `Potency` across some `Plan`s, with no `Plan` worsened, under given `Circumstances` `C`.

> `Evolution<P,Q,C> :: Potency<p,Q,C> ≥ Potency<p,P,C>, |{ x -> (Potency<x,Q,C> > Potency<x,P,C>)[x] }| > 0`

---

Adaptation *(Gart)*
: a change of `Paradigm` `P` into `Q` tracking a change of `Circumstances` `C` into `D`, so that `Potency` is preserved within a tolerance `ε`.

> `Adaptation<P,Q,C,D,ε> :: Potency<Q,D> ≥ Potency<P,C> − ε`

---

Stagnation *(Zastoj)*
: persistence of `Paradigm` `P` despite `Circumstances` shifting from `C` to `D`, with strictly declining `Potency`.

> `Stagnation<P,C,D> :: Potency<P,D> < Potency<P,C>`

---

Regression *(Adkat\*)*
: a change of `Paradigm` `P` itself into `Q` toward lower `Potency` under unchanged `Circumstances` `C`.

> `Regression<P,Q,C> :: Potency<Q,C> < Potency<P,C>`

## Branching

Crisis *(Zlam\*)*
: a `State` at a [`Bifurcation`](02-process.md#landmarks-of-a-flow): the existing `Paradigm` `P` is inadequate to current `Circumstances` `C` — its `Potency` is at most a tolerance `ε` — AND no replacement `Paradigm` in the `Set` `A` of attainable ones does better.

> `Crisis<P,C,A,ε> :: Potency<P,C> ≤ ε ∧ |A ∩ { x -> (Potency<x,C> > Potency<P,C>)[x] }| < 1`

*NOTE*: the branching point: either [`Breakthrough`](16-limits.md#the-ceiling) yields a new `Paradigm`, or the `Agent`'s capacity to participate in the `System` collapses.
